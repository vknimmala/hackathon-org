# Landing Feature

Owns SurgeVector Hackathon Phase 1 event content, hero sections, registration calls to action, and participant guidance cards.

Keep this feature participant-facing. The landing page should explain the internal hackathon, idea-first participation flow, team formation rules, volunteer path, and mentor support without presenting the app as a hackathon organizing product.

Visual guidance:

- Use a light cream or white event canvas for the landing route.
- Keep orange, black, and white as the primary palette, with orange gradients used as accents.
- Use subtle glassmorphism through translucent white or cream surfaces, soft orange borders, and restrained shadows.
- Keep messaging event-focused and Phase 1 scoped; do not introduce future-phase product capabilities.
